Who negotiates what

What each of the brothers took to Washington, according to public records.

Flávio Bolsonaro

Senator (PL, Rio de Janeiro), presidential candidate. At least six trips to the US in 2026.

  • Asked Trump and Rubio to designate PCC and CV as terrorists. The designation came out the day after the meeting.May 27–28, 2026
  • Offered a "long-term strategic partnership" in rare earths and critical minerals.White House, May 2026
  • Proposed to the USTR a legislative commitment that Pix would not connect to "non-Western" payment systems.Document of Jul 1, 2026
  • Asked that the 25% surcharge be postponed by 180 days, until after the election.USTR, Jul 2026
  • His government plan coincides with 16 of the 21 American demands, according to an ICL survey.Revealed in Sep 2026
  • Says he receives no help from the American government and that he will keep Pix free of charge.

Eduardo Bolsonaro

Former federal deputy. Has lived in the US since February 2025.

  • Said he was "focused 24 hours a day" on pressing American authorities against Supreme Court decisions.
  • Celebrated the Magnitsky Act sanctions against Alexandre de Moraes and said it was "just the beginning".Jul 2025
  • According to the Prosecutor General's Office, he worked with Paulo Figueiredo to obtain sanctions against officials and tariffs against Brazil.
  • Suggested replacing Pix with Zelle, an American system.Jun 2026
  • Accompanied Flávio at the meetings with Rubio and Vance.May 2026
  • Lost his seat for absences and was sentenced by the Supreme Court to 4 years and 2 months for coercion during legal proceedings.Dec 2025 · Jun 2026

Rubio's agenda for the region

What the Secretary of State and the Trump administration do and say about Latin America.

Doctrine

The National Security Strategy of December 2025 created the "Trump Corollary" to the Monroe Doctrine. In August 2026, the State Department called the doctrine a "central pillar" and quoted Trump: American dominance in the Western Hemisphere "will never be questioned again".

Military force

Capture of Nicolás Maduro in January 2026 and about 200 dead in strikes on boats in the Caribbean and the Pacific. Afterwards, an oil deal with Venezuela's interim government.

Criminal factions

Cartels classified as "narco-terrorists", which has included PCC and CV since May 2026. The Lula government opposes it, fearing it could justify American military action in Brazil.

Allies

Support for "iron fist" and aligned governments: Milei in Argentina, Abelardo de la Espriella in Colombia. In September 2026, Rubio visited Colombia, Peru and Ecuador.

Brazil

Tariffs; revoked visas for Moraes, Jorge Messias and the Brazilian ambassador; and the nomination of Daniel Perez, nicknamed "Mini Rubio", as ambassador in Brasília. Gazeta do Povo describes an effort to isolate countries seen as too close to China.

Coincidence or strategy?

The convergence between Washington and Flávio's campaign is no accident. It is public and acknowledged by both sides. But there is a difference between open alignment and coordinated interference, and that second part has not yet been proven.

What is proven

  • The Trump administration pressures Brazil with tariffs, sanctions and visas, and mentioned Jair Bolsonaro and the election in its demands.
  • Flávio took proposals to Trump, Rubio, Vance and the USTR that meet American demands.
  • Flávio's requests became American measures in less than 24 hours, in the case of PCC and CV.
  • Eduardo was convicted in Brazil for using this pressure to intimidate the Supreme Court.
  • Meta adopted rhetoric aligned with Trump against Latin American courts.

What is not yet proven

  • That Meta moderated Brazilian content at the request of the American government.
  • That foreign money or structures are financing Flávio's campaign. The PT asked the Supreme Court to investigate.
  • That the group linked to Peter Thiel in São Paulo had an electoral purpose.
  • That Palantir or Alex Karp have any role in the election.
